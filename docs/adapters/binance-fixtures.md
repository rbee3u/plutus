# Binance interface sample inventory

Read the [shared contract and workflow](./fixtures.md) and [mapping rules](./binance.md). The library currently contains 35 **documented** responses and **zero recorded responses**. All old recordings were removed; no new-format real capture, owner acceptance or freeze is claimed. Inline boundary/error overrides are synthetic.

Documented mapping scope: Classic Trading and Portfolio Margin; UM Single/Multi-Assets, linear/inverse perpetual and delivery mapping, nonempty Funding, margin borrowing, Flexible/Locked Earn and CALL/PUT Dual principal. Tests retain pagination, malformed values, incomplete products, failure isolation and error classification. Metadata proves instrument mapping, never a nonempty real holding.

## Acquisition status

Every interface below awaits real evidence under ADR 0036. Record one selected request only after agreeing its concrete account preparation. First acquire Binance; OKX, Bybit and Kraken follow independently. Future preparation distinguishes request/mapping variants from unrelated account settings; coexisting products can share a response. Permission/product availability, transient settlement states and difficult pagination boundaries remain explicit real evidence gaps until observed. No account preparation or exchange request was performed during this rebuild.
