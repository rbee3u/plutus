Every linked `testdata/` file remains independently constructed `synthetic` data. Tests do not consume evidence or change when a new recording is accepted.

Current scope is Separate wallets and Unified Wallet, Spot/Spot Margin, the Futures WS wallet snapshot, supported futures contracts and Earn/Auto Earn. Spot and Futures use separate read-only credential groups. The accepted Unified round covers nonzero Main, Coin-M and Holding wallets, linear perpetual/delivery and inverse perpetual positions, and instant/bonded/timed Earn. Separate-wallet evidence and other unobserved states remain explicit gaps. The owner has only one Unified account and requests no further account adjustments; do not require new preparation to close these gaps. There is no supported Kraken Dual Investment interface.

## Acceptance and gaps

Synthetic replay proves deliberate request/field/mapping/failure behavior. It does not prove exchange access, current product availability, account ownership, nonempty real coverage or a real asset total. Accepted evidence entries name the exact file in `evidence/`, actual UTC recording date, owner-declared versus directly observed settings, critical shapes, document discrepancies, trimming/redaction and replacement reasons. Generic endpoints are not restricted to the structure used to sample them.

Acquire only explicit prepared requests, with compatible holdings combined and incompatible settings separated. Do not require artificial real transient states, bulk positions or pagination solely to reproduce a test scene. Preserve unavailable critical evidence as a named gap and continue other interfaces.

### Critical shapes and documentation observations

- The WS response has nonzero USD/USDT Main quantities and account measures, a nonzero `F-ETH:USD` Coin-M wallet, and nonzero USDT/XRP Holding amounts. `cross` and `isolated.PF_ETHUSD` coexist; `isolated_list` is also present although not listed on the inspected [balances documentation](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/api-reference/futures-websocket/balances) page. Original wallet totals, timestamp, sequence, PnL, funding and margin fields are preserved.
- The positions are `PF_DOGEUSD` long size `10`, `PF_ETHUSD` long size `0.002` with `maxFixedLeverage: 9.0`, `FF_ETHUSD_261002` short size `0.01`, and `PI_ETHUSD` short size `20`. All four have matching contract metadata and mark-price ticker rows. The FF contract is `flexible_futures`, expires `2026-10-02T08:00:00Z`, and belongs to Unified Main; its delivery expiry does not route it to Coin-M. All four contract sizes are `1`.
- Every returned position `fillTime` is `1970-01-01T00:00:00.000Z`; preserve the placeholder instead of claiming a real entry time. Flexible positions include `pnlCurrency: USD`; PI ETH omits it. FF funding is explicit `null`; perpetual funding is numeric, including negative values. These are interface observations, not reasons to invent a missing currency or time. The existing mapper leaves inverse position PnL unset without a declared settlement currency and does not map fillTime.
- Four nonzero USDT Earn allocations reference retained strategies: instant, bonded with two unbonding allocations, six-month timed, and eighteen-month timed. Native totals, converted values, reward figures, utilization, original payout/creation/expiry times and nested allocations remain unchanged. `amount_allocated.timed` supplements the states described in [the allocation documentation](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/earn/list-earn-allocations); existing mapping reads `amount_allocated.total.native`.
- The [strategies parameter schema](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/earn/list-earn-strategies) enumerates `timed` while its introductory lock-type explanation lists only flex/instant/bonded. The real response includes all four types. Both responses have `next_cursor: null`; their complete first-page responses are consistent with the documentation's current unpaged behavior, not proof of a pagination boundary. A listed flex strategy is metadata, not nonzero Auto Earn coverage.
- The instruments request uses the production parameter `contractType=all`, so one option metadata row is retained. No option position was present; Kraken option support is unchanged. FI inverse delivery metadata is likewise not evidence of a nonempty delivery position. No `futures_vanilla` instrument was returned in this captured list.
