# Bybit interface sample inventory

Read the [shared contract and workflow](./fixtures.md) and [mapping rules](./bybit.md). The library currently contains 39 **documented** responses and **zero recorded responses**. All old recordings were removed; no new-format real capture, owner acceptance or freeze is claimed. Inline boundary/error overrides are synthetic.

Documented mapping scope: Global UTA 2.0 Isolated/Cross/PM, linear USDT/USDC and inverse contracts, Funding, Flexible/Fixed-Term Savings, BuyLow/SellHigh Dual and refusals. Tests retain pagination, malformed values, incomplete products, failure isolation and error classification. Metadata proves instrument mapping, never a nonempty real holding.

## Acquisition status

Every interface below awaits real evidence under ADR 0036. Record one selected request only after agreeing its concrete account preparation. First acquire Binance; OKX, Bybit and Kraken follow independently. Future preparation distinguishes request/mapping variants from unrelated account settings; coexisting products can share a response. Permission/product availability, transient settlement states and difficult pagination boundaries remain explicit real evidence gaps until observed. No account preparation or exchange request was performed during this rebuild.
