## Acceptance and gaps

Synthetic replay proves deliberate request/field/mapping/failure behavior. It does not prove exchange access, current product availability, account ownership, nonempty real coverage or a real asset total. Frozen evidence entries below name the exact file in `evidence/`, actual UTC recording date, owner-declared versus directly observed settings, critical shapes, document discrepancies and trimming/redaction. Replacements require a coverage-gap, interface-change or error-fix reason. Generic endpoints are not restricted to the structure used to sample them.

Acquire only explicit prepared requests, with compatible holdings combined and incompatible settings separated. Do not require artificial real transient states, bulk positions or pagination solely to reproduce a test scene. Preserve unavailable critical evidence as a named gap and continue other interfaces.
